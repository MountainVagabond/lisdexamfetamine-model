Model assumptions

  • One-compartment model, first-order absorption (ka 0.7 /h), peak about 3.5–4 h after the dose.
  • Half-life log-normal: median 10.73 h, mean 11.0 h; SD variants 2 h (σ 0.180, μ 2.382), 2.5 h (σ 0.224, μ 2.373), 3 h (σ 0.268, μ 2.362).
  • Volume of distribution 3.2 L/kg; 29.48% of the dose by weight becomes d-amphetamine.
  • Daily use assumes the same dose time every 24 h, at steady state.
  • Only half-life varies between the lines. Real variation, for example from urine pH and kidney function, is wider. Illustrative only, not for dosing decisions.
